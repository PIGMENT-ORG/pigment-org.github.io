Frequently Asked Questions

General

What is PIGMENT?

PIGMENT is an API that uses genetic algorithms to generate artwork. Images evolve through generations of mutation and selection.

How does it work?

We represent images as collections of colored polygons. Through genetic algorithms, these polygons evolve to match target images or create new artwork based on prompts.

Pricing

Is there a free tier?

Yes! Free tier includes 60 requests/minute and 2 concurrent jobs.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and cryptocurrency.

Technical

What image formats are supported?

JPEG, PNG, WebP, and SVG output.

How long do jobs take?

Typically 30-60 seconds for 1000 generations, depending on image size.

Can I use my own images?

Yes! Provide a publicly accessible URL or base64 encoded image.

Limits

What's the maximum image size?

10MB per image, maximum dimensions 2048×2048 pixels.

How long are images stored?

Generated images are stored for 30 days on our CDN.
