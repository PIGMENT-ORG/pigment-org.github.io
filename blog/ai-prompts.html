AI Prompts Guide Evolution

By PIGMENT Team March 5, 2026 8 min read

"Make it glitchy." "Add more contrast." "Give it an organic feel." With PIGMENT v6, you can guide evolution using natural language — and watch as your words transform into actual mutations.

The Challenge: From Words to Mutations

Evolution is a blind process — it doesn't understand "glitchy" or "organic." It only understands mutations and fitness. So how do we bridge the gap between human language and genetic algorithms?

The answer is a two-layer system:

  1. Claude API (when available) — Translates prompts into structured mutation plans
  2. Keyword fallback — Intelligent local matching when Claude is unavailable
